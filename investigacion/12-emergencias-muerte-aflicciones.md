You gain the staggered condition and can only take a single move or standard action each turn [...] if you perform any standard action (or any other strenuous action) you take 1 point of damage after completing the act. Unless your activity increased your hit points, you are now at –1 hit points and dying." | Un aliado a 0 puede curarse a sí mismo (acción estándar) y el punto de daño posterior no lo tumba si la cura lo subió. |
| **Dying (−1 a −(Con−1))** | "If your hit point total is negative, but not equal to or greater than your Constitution score, you're dying. **Coup de grace** (https://www.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?Name=Helpless%20Defenders&Category=Combat%20Modifiers, Core pg. 197): "As a full-round action, you can use a melee weapon to deliver a coup de grace to a helpless opponent. [...] You automatically hit and score a critical hit. If the defender survives the damage, he must make a Fortitude save (DC 10 + damage dealt) or die. [...] Delivering a coup de grace provokes attacks of opportunity from threatening opponents. You can't deliver a coup de grace against a creature that is immune to critical hits." → Un aliado moribundo o estable-inconsciente adyacente a un enemigo inteligente **muere en el siguiente asalto completo del enemigo** salvo que alguien lo suba a 0+ o lo aleje. Ni *death ward* ("does not protect against other sorts of attacks, even if those attacks might be lethal", Core pg. 264) ni Hard to Kill lo evitan; **Immortal** (tier 9) confirma el diseño: "This ability doesn't apply if you're killed by a coup de grace or critical hit performed by either a mythic creature..." (MA pg. 12).

**Stabilize** (https://www.aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Stabilize, Core pg. 348): orison, 1 estándar, V S, **close (25 + 5/2 niveles = 50 pies a CL 11)**, "you target a living creature that has –1 or fewer hit points. That creature is automatically stabilized and does not lose any further hit points. If the creature later takes damage, it continues dying normally." Sin tirada de ataque (Will negates (harmless)). Es la forma gratuita de frenar a un moribundo a 50 pies; no lo despierta.

**Rebuke Death (Sp)** (https://www.aonprd.com/DomainDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Healing, Core pg. 44): "You can touch a living creature as a standard action, healing it for 1d4 points of damage plus 1 for every two cleric levels you possess [1d4+5 a nivel 11]. You can only use this ability on a creature that is below 0 hit points. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier [10/día]." Es (Sp): provoca AdO y admite lanzar a la defensiva (Table 8-2, "Use spell-like ability: Yes"). Estabiliza; solo despierta si el aliado estaba entre −1 y −8 (con 7,5 de media).

**Heal skill, First Aid** (https://www.aonprd.com/SkillDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Heal, Core pg. 98): DC 15, acción estándar, "First aid also stops a character from losing hit points due to effects that cause bleed"; **provoca** (Table 8-2 "Stabilize a dying friend: Yes"). Solo si no queda ni un orison.

### 1.2 Capa mítica: Hard to Kill y la pregunta del umbral de Breath of Life

**Hard to Kill (Ex)** (https://www.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=1618, MA pg. 12): "Whenever you're below 0 hit points, you automatically stabilize without needing to attempt a Constitution check. If you have an ability that allows you to act while below 0 hit points, you still lose hit points for taking actions, as specified by that ability. Bleed damage still causes you to lose hit points when below 0 hit points. In addition, you don't die until your total number of negative hit points is equal to or greater than double your Constitution score."

Cuenta de umbrales: Con 14 → muere a **−28**; Con 16 → **−32**; Con 18 → **−36**; Con 20 → **−40**. Todos los PJ del grupo son míticos → todos usan −2×Con. El bleed sigue restando bajo 0 (y puede llevar a −2×Con): frenarlo con cualquier cura de pg o Heal DC 15 (Conditions pg. 565: "Bleeding can be stopped by a DC 15 Heal check or through the application of any spell that cures hit point damage").

**Breath of Life** (https://www.aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Breath%20of%20Life, Core pg. 251), texto literal: "This spell cures 5d8 points of damage + 1 point per caster level (maximum +25). [...] If cast upon a creature that has died within 1 round, apply the healing from this spell to the creature. If the healed creature's hit point total is at a negative amount less than its Constitution score, it comes back to life and stabilizes at its new hit point total. If the creature's hit point total is at a negative amount equal to or greater than its Constitution score, the creature remains dead. Creatures brought back to life through breath of life gain a temporary negative level that lasts for 1 day. Creatures slain by death effects cannot be saved by breath of life."

**Mythic Breath of Life** (misma URL, MA pg. 87): "The spell heals 5d12 points of damage + 1 point per caster level (maximum +25). It can revive a creature that died of hp damage within the past 2 rounds. If the target was slain by a death effect that allows a saving throw, this spell allows the creature to attempt another save against that effect. If this save is successful, the creature is revived with –10 hp plus the amount healed by this spell." Requiere 1 poder mítico y conocer el conjuro mítico (Mythic Spell Lore: "You can learn a number of mythic spells equal to your tier", https://www.aonprd.com/MythicFeatDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Mythic%20Spell%20Lore, MA pg. 70 → 3 conjuros a tier 3).

**¿Sigue siendo −Con el umbral de Breath of Life sobre un aliado mítico que muere a −2×Con?** Búsqueda: la FAQ de AoN (https://www.aonprd.com/FAQs.aspx) y paizo.com **no tienen FAQ**; el hilo https://paizo.com/threads/rzs2qcem plantea exactamente esa interacción y no tiene respuesta de staff. → **Decisión del GM.** Las dos lecturas:

- **Lectura A (literal):** *breath of life* dice "less than its Constitution score"; Hard to Kill solo cambia cuándo se muere, no el texto del conjuro. Para revivir a un mítico hay que dejarlo por encima de −Con (p. ej. −15 o más con Con 16), aunque vivo estaría estable a −25.
- **Lectura B (funcional):** el conjuro codifica "el umbral de muerte de la criatura", que para un mítico es −2×Con; basta con quedar por encima de −2×Con. Apoyo textual indirecto: Relentless Healing (MA pg. 35) usa la redacción genérica "above the threshold for death" y dice funcionar "similar to the way breath of life functions".

**Ejemplos numéricos** (tanque mítico Con 16: vivo hasta −31, muerto a −32 o menos; *breath of life* cura 5d8+11):

| pg del tanque muerto | Lectura A (debe quedar ≥ −15) | Lectura B (debe quedar ≥ −31) | Mythic BoL (5d12+11), A / B |
|---|---|---|---|
| −32 (murió justo) | necesita ≥ 17 de cura: mínimo 16... 5d8 ≥ 6 → **100 %** | **100 %** | 100 % / 100 % |
| −35 | 5d8 ≥ 9 → **99,8 %** | 100 % | 100 % / 100 % |
| −40 | 5d8 ≥ 14 → **96 %** | 100 % | 99,5 % / 100 % |
| −45 | 5d8 ≥ 19 → **78 %** | 100 % | 97 % / 100 % |
| −50 | 5d8 ≥ 24 → **42 %** | 99,9 % | 87 % / 100 % |
| −55 | 5d8 ≥ 29 → **12,5 %** | 97,6 % | 69 % / 99,7 % |
| −60 | 5d8 ≥ 34 → **1,4 %** | 83 % | 45 % / 97,5 % |

El ejemplo del enunciado (tanque Con 16 a −30 y BoL de 33,5): a −30 el tanque mítico **no está muerto** (−30 > −32); BoL simplemente lo cura a +3,5: consciente y funcional. Para un aliado **no mítico** (muere a −16): de −30 necesita quedar ≥ −15 → cura ≥ 15 → 5d8 ≥ 4 → 100 % (mínimo 16). La diferencia entre lecturas solo importa cuando el golpe mortal fue enorme (−45 o peor): ahí conviene **Relentless Healing + heal** (110) en lugar de BoL, que resuelve las dos lecturas a la vez (−60 + 110 = +50).

**Relentless Healing (Su)** (https://www.aonprd.com/PathAbilities.aspx?Path=Hierophant, MA pg. 35, tier 1): "If a creature has died within 1 round, as a free action you can expend one use of mythic power to apply healing magic to that creature. This healing can be from a spell or effect you cause or from a magic item you wield. If this healing brings the creature's hit points above the threshold for death, it comes back to life and stabilizes at its new hit point total (similar to the way breath of life functions); otherwise, it remains dead. Alternatively, you can expend two uses of mythic power on a dead creature that would have the ability to magically heal itself if it were alive (such as a dead cleric with a prepared cure light wounds spell) in order to trigger the most powerful healing magic it knows or has prepared." Lo que hace: convierte **cualquier** curación del clérigo (*heal* 110, CCW 43,5, un channel 21, una varita de CLW 9,5) en un *breath of life* por 1 poder mítico y **sin gastar acción** (la acción es la de la cura). Lo que **no** dice: nada sobre nivel negativo (el paréntesis "similar to the way breath of life functions" deja al GM aplicarlo o no) y nada sobre efectos de muerte (BoL sí los excluye) → **dos decisiones del GM**. "Threshold for death" respeta de forma natural el −2×Con de Hard to Kill.

**Cheat Death (Hero Points)** (https://www.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?Name=Hero%20Points&Category=Optional%20Rule%20Systems, APG pg. 323): "A character can spend 2 hero points to cheat death. [...] generally the character is left alive, with negative hit points but stable. [...] Cheating death is the only way for a character to spend more than 1 hero point in a turn. The character can spend hero points in this way to prevent the death of a familiar, animal companion, eidolon, or special mount, **but not another character or NPC**." → cada PJ solo puede salvarse a sí mismo; el clérigo no puede "comprar" la vida de un tanque con sus puntos. Sí puede: **Act Out of Turn** (1 punto: "take your turn immediately. Treat this as a readied action [...] You may only take a move or a standard action on this turn" → un *breath of life* fuera de turno, en el mismo asalto en que cayó el aliado), **Extra Action** (1 punto: "an additional standard or move action this turn" — sin la restricción "no conjuros" de Amazing Initiative), **Bonus** (+8 antes / +4 después de tirar, a una d20 propia: sirve para una prueba de CL o de concentración), **Recall** (1 punto: "recall a spell you have already cast" → un segundo *heal* o *breath of life* del mismo slot).

### 1.3 Raise Dead y lo que queda más allá del nivel 11

**Raise Dead** (https://www.aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Raise%20Dead, Core pg. 329): cleric 5; **1 minuto**; "M (diamond worth 5,000 gp)"; toque; "You can raise a creature that has been dead for no longer than 1 day per caster level [11 días]. In addition, the subject's soul must be free and willing to return." Costes literales: "The subject of the spell gains two permanent negative levels when it is raised [...] A character who died with spells prepared has a 50% chance of losing any given spell upon being raised. [...] A raised creature has a number of hit points equal to its current HD [11]. Any ability scores damaged to 0 are raised to 1. Normal poison and normal disease are cured in the process of raising the subject, but magical diseases and curses are not undone. [...] the body of the creature to be raised must be whole. Otherwise, missing parts are still missing when the creature is brought back to life. [...] A creature who has been turned into an undead creature or killed by a death effect can't be raised by this spell. Constructs, elementals, outsiders, and undead creatures can't be raised. The spell cannot bring back a creature that has died of old age."

Consecuencias que suelen olvidarse:
- **Efectos de muerte** (*slay living*, *finger of death*, *phantasmal killer*, *wail of the banshee*, *circle of death*, *destruction*): ni *breath of life* ni *raise dead*. Solo **Resurrection** (cleric 7, "M (diamond worth 10,000 gp)", "It can even bring back creatures whose bodies have been destroyed [...] the creature cannot have been dead longer than 10 years per caster level [...] one permanent negative level", y **sí** funciona sobre muertos por efectos de muerte; https://www.aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Resurrection, Core pg. 334) o *true resurrection* (9.º). **Un Cleric 11 no lanza 7.º** (la tabla de clase da 6.º a nivel 11 y 7.º a nivel 13: https://www.aonprd.com/ClassDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Cleric). **Reincarnate** es druid 4 / hunter 4 / medium 4 / shaman 4 / witch 5, **no cleric**, exige 1 semana, oils 1.000 po, 2 niveles negativos permanentes y tampoco funciona vs death effects (https://www.aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Reincarnate, Core pg. 331).
- **Los 2 niveles negativos permanentes**: Restoration (cleric 4, 3 asaltos) los quita "one permanent negative level" por lanzamiento con "diamond dust worth 1,000 gp" y "cannot be used to dispel more than one permanent negative level possessed by a target in a 1-week period" (https://www.aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Restoration, Core pg. 334) → **2 semanas y 2.000 po** además del diamante de 5.000 → **7.000 po y 15 días** por un *raise dead* completo, contra 0 po y 0 días de un *breath of life* a tiempo. Mientras tanto el aliado tiene −2 a ataques, salvaciones, pruebas, CMB/CMD, −10 pg máximos y actuales y lanza como 2 niveles menos (Core pg. 562). Nota brutal: **pg al revivir = DG (11) − 10 por los dos niveles negativos = 1 pg**: hay que curarlo antes de moverse.
- **Mummy rot**: "Anyone who dies from mummy rot turns to dust and cannot be raised without a resurrection or greater magic" (Bestiary pg. 210) → sin cuerpo entero, *raise dead* no sirve (ver 2.d).
- **Tiempo**: 11 días; **Gentle Repose** (cleric 2, 1 estándar, "corpse touched", "1 day/level": https://www.aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Gentle%20Repose, Core pg. 289) preserva el cuerpo y "days spent under the influence of this spell don't count against the time limit" de *raise dead*: 1 ranura de 2.º compra 11 días más por lanzamiento. Llevar 1 pergamino (2 × 3 × 25 = **150 po**).
- **Saber si está muerto o moribundo**: la regla no lo revela automáticamente. **Deathwatch** (cleric 1, cono de 30 pies, 10 min/nivel = 110 min: https://www.aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Deathwatch, Core pg. 265) distingue "dead", "fragile (alive and wounded, with 3 or fewer hit points left)", "fighting off death (4 or more hit points)". Preguntar al GM si acepta que el jugador conozca los pg del aliado (mesa habitual: sí).

**Pergaminos de 7.º para un CL 11** (https://www.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?Name=Scrolls&Category=Magic%20Items, Core pg. 490): "The price of a scroll is equal to the level of the spell × the creator's caster level × 25 gp. If the scroll has a material component cost, it is added to the base price". Un pergamino de 7.º se escribe a CL 13 mínimo (Table 15-15). "If she meets all three requirements but her own caster level is lower than the scroll spell's caster level, then she has to make a caster level check (DC = scroll's caster level + 1) to cast the spell successfully. If she fails, she must make a DC 5 Wisdom check to avoid a mishap [...] A natural roll of 1 always fails [...] If the caster level check fails but no mishap occurs, the scroll is not expended." → DC 14 con 1d20+11: falla con 1 y 2 → **90 %**; con Surge (1d6 tras ver la tirada) → 99 %; con Bonus de Hero Point (+8 antes) → solo falla el 1 natural (95 %). Un fallo sin mishap no consume el pergamino (se reintenta). Precios: **Resurrection** 7 × 13 × 25 = 2.275 + 10.000 = **12.275 po**; **Regenerate** **2.275 po**; **Greater Restoration** 2.275 + 5.000 = **7.275 po**; **Raise Dead** 5 × 9 × 25 = 1.125 + 5.000 = **6.125 po**. Alternativa: **clérigo NPC** (https://www.aonprd.com/EquipmentMiscDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Spellcasting, Core pg. 159 / UE pg. 99: "caster level × spell level × 10 gp" más componentes; un lanzador de 7.º solo es esperable en una **metrópolis**): *resurrection* 13 × 7 × 10 = 910 + 10.000 = **10.910 po**; *regenerate* **910 po**; *greater restoration* 910 + 5.000 = **5.910 po**; *stone to flesh* (arcano CL 11, gran ciudad) 11 × 6 × 10 = **660 po**.

### 1.5 PROTOCOLO POR ASALTOS: "un aliado cae"

Datos a conocer: Con del aliado (umbral −2×Con si es mítico), pg actuales, cuántos asaltos hace que murió, causa (pg / death effect / Con 0 / niveles negativos ≥ DG), si el aliado o el clérigo están amenazados, distancia.

**Asalto 0 — el golpe que lo tumba (todavía es el turno del enemigo):**
1. Si el que cae es **el clérigo**: Reactive Healing (inmediata, 1 channel, +21/28) si el golpe no supera el margen; si aun así queda bajo 0, Hard to Kill lo estabiliza solo; si el daño lo mataría (≤ −28 con Con 14), **Cheat Death** (2 Hero Points) → vivo, negativo y estable.
2. Si cae **un aliado** y el clérigo tiene 1 Hero Point: **Act Out of Turn** (turno inmediato, 1 estándar): *breath of life* (5.º) o *heal* + Relentless Healing (1 poder) sin esperar a que el enemigo termine su ataque completo o haga coup de grace. Sin Hero Points: esperar al turno propio (el aliado moribundo pierde 1 pg/asalto y tira Con; el mítico ni eso).

**Asalto 1 — turno del clérigo (aliado caído hace < 1 asalto):**
1. ¿Está muerto? Si el GM no lo dice y no hay *deathwatch* activo, usar una acción que sirva en ambos casos: *heal* con Relentless Healing "por si acaso" (si estaba vivo, el poder mítico no se gasta: la regla solo se activa "if a creature has died within 1 round"; confirmar con el GM que el gasto es condicional).
2. **Moribundo/estable, no amenazado**: Stabilize (orison, 50 pies) si solo hay que frenar la hemorragia y sobran cosas más urgentes; CCW/heal a 30 pies si tiene que volver a pegar este asalto.
3. **Moribundo/estable y amenazado por un enemigo que puede hacer coup de grace**: prioridad absoluta llevarlo a **0+** (consciente = no helpless): *heal* (110) o CCW (43,5) a 30 pies; si el clérigo también está amenazado y no puede dar un paso de 5 pies, lanzar a la defensiva (DC 27 para *heal*, 23 para CCW; concentración +18 → 60 % / 80 %; con Surge 78 % / 95 %) o usar **channel** (Su: "does not provoke", Core pg. 40 y Table 8-2) que cura 21 sin tiradas y llega a 30 pies.
4. **Muerto por pg, hace ≤ 1 asalto**: (a) *heal* + Relentless Healing: 1 ranura de 6.º + 1 poder, funciona con cualquiera de las dos lecturas del umbral, sin nivel negativo (GM), acción estándar; (b) *breath of life*: 1 ranura de 5.º, 0 poder, 1 nivel negativo temporal, probabilidad según tabla 1.2 (100 % si murió por ≤ 8 pg de margen; 78 % desde −45 con lectura A); (c) CCW + Relentless Healing (43,5 → 98 % desde −45 con lectura A): ranura de 4.º + 1 poder; (d) sin ranuras: channel + Relentless Healing (21) o Channeled Revival (3 channels, full-round, provoca). Con Faith's Reach todo esto a 30 pies sin moverse (lectura del toque a distancia: ver Sección 3).
5. **Muerto por efecto de muerte**: BoL normal **no**. *Mythic breath of life* (1 poder + conjuro mítico conocido + ranura de 5.º): nueva salvación al aliado; si la pasa, vive a −10 + 43,5 = +33,5 de media. Relentless Healing: el texto no lo prohíbe → **GM**. Si el GM dice que no: el cuerpo va a *resurrection* (pergamino 12.275 po, prueba DC 14 al 90 %, o NPC en una metrópolis) — **imposible con recursos propios de nivel 11**.
6. **Muerto por Con 0 (drenaje/daño) o por niveles negativos ≥ DG**: *breath of life* dice "died of hp damage" (versión mítica) / "hit point total" (base): por texto **no cubre** estas muertes; el drenaje de Con a 0 tampoco es un "death effect" (FAQ: "Energy drain is not a death effect", https://www.aonprd.com/FAQs.aspx) → *raise dead* sí funciona sobre ellos ("Any ability scores damaged to 0 are raised to 1"), pero con niveles negativos permanentes iguales a los DG hay que lanzar *restoration* "the round after it is restored to life" (Core pg. 562). Prevención: **Death Ward** (4.º, 11 minutos, "immune to energy drain and any negative energy effects"; https://www.aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Death%20Ward).

**Asalto 2 (muerto hace 2 asaltos):** solo *mythic breath of life* ("within the past 2 rounds"). **Asaltos 3-10 (≤ 1 minuto):** nada en combate (Gift of Life del subdominio Resurrection reemplaza Healer's Blessing: descartado en el frente 02). **Después del combate:** *gentle repose* si no hay diamante a mano; *raise dead* (5.000 po, 1 minuto, 11 días de plazo) → el aliado vuelve con 1 pg efectivo, 2 niveles negativos permanentes y 50 % de perder cada conjuro preparado → *restoration* 1.000 po en el acto y otra a los 7 días. Death effect / mummy rot / cuerpo destruido: **resurrection** (pergamino 12.275 po con prueba DC 14, o NPC 10.910 po). Presupuestar el diamante de 5.000 **antes** de la primera sesión: sin diamante, *raise dead* no existe.

## SECCIÓN 2 — AFLICCIONES QUE HEAL NO CURA

**Lo que *heal* sí cura** (https://www.aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Heal, Core pg. 294): "ability damage, blinded, confused, dazed, dazzled, deafened, diseased, exhausted, fatigued, feebleminded, insanity, nauseated, poisoned, sickened, and stunned" y 110 pg. **Lo que no:** "Heal does not remove negative levels or restore permanently drained ability score points." Tampoco: petrificación, maldiciones, parálisis, miedo, dominación/posesión, staggered, miembros perdidos, muerte. **Mythic Heal** (MA pg. 97): 165 pg y "Augmented: If you expend two uses of mythic power, the spell also acts as restoration" → **la única *restoration* de 1 acción estándar** que existe a nivel 11 (2 poderes + conjuro mítico conocido + ranura de 6.º): quita todos los niveles negativos temporales o **uno** permanente (¿exige el polvo de diamante de 1.000 po? El texto de *mythic heal* no lo dice; **GM**).

Regla general de las aflicciones (https://www.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?Name=Afflictions&Category=Special%20Abilities, Core pg. 555): "Hit point damage and ability score damage is not removed when an affliction is cured. Such damage must be healed normally." y "Hit point and ability score damage caused by an affliction cannot be healed naturally while the affliction persists." → quitar la aflicción y después curar el daño son **dos** acciones.

Tres herramientas míticas transversales a toda la sección:
- **Eldritch Breach (Su)** (Hierophant tier 1, MA pg. 34): "When attempting a caster level check to dispel an effect, overcome spell resistance, or otherwise determine whether or not your magic affects a target (such as with knock or neutralize poison), roll twice and take the higher result." Gratis y permanente; cubre *break enchantment*, *remove curse*, *remove disease*, *neutralize poison*, *dispel magic* y la prueba DC 20 de mummy rot.
- **Recalled Blessing (Su)** (Divine Surge, MA pg. 32; alternativa a Inspired Spell): "cast any one divine spell without expending a prepared spell or spell slot. If you prepare spells, this spell must be one you prepared today [...] If the spell heals damage or requires you to attempt a caster level check to cure an affliction or remove a condition, roll twice and take the higher result." **Sin la restricción de "1 standard action"** → sirve para Restoration (3 asaltos) y Break Enchantment (1 minuto) si se prepararon ese día. Inspired Spell (CL +2, cualquier conjuro de la lista, pero solo de 1 acción estándar o menos) **no** sirve para Restoration ni Break Enchantment.

### 2.a Niveles negativos y drenaje de energía

**Regla** (https://www.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?Name=Energy%20Drain%20and%20Negative%20Levels&Category=Special%20Abilities, Core pg. 562): "For each negative level a creature has, it takes a cumulative –1 penalty on all ability checks, attack rolls, combat maneuver checks, Combat Maneuver Defense, saving throws, and skill checks. In addition, the creature reduces its current and total hit points by 5 for each negative level it possesses. The creature is also treated as one level lower for the purpose of level-dependent variables (such as spellcasting) for each negative level possessed. Spellcasters do not lose any prepared spells or slots as a result of negative levels. If a creature's negative levels equal or exceed its total Hit Dice, it dies. A creature with temporary negative levels receives a new saving throw to remove the negative level each day. The DC of this save is the same as the effect that caused the negative levels." Permanentes: "treated just like temporary negative levels, but they do not allow a new save each day to remove them. Level drain can be removed through spells like restoration."

**Cuándo se vuelven permanentes** (UMR Energy Drain, https://www.aonprd.com/UMR.aspx?ItemName=Energy%20Drain, Bestiary pg. 299): "Negative levels remain until 24 hours have passed or until they are removed with a spell such as restoration. If a negative level is not removed before 24 hours have passed, the affected creature must attempt a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + 1/2 draining creature's racial HD + draining creature's Charisma modifier [...]). On a success, the negative level goes away with no harm to the creature. On a failure, the negative level becomes permanent. A separate saving throw is required for each negative level." FAQ oficial 2017/01/07 (https://www.aonprd.com/FAQs.aspx): la regla del Core (nueva salvación cada día) es la general; "almost every specific rule in the game functions differently, and specific overrides general" y aclara que antes de las 24 h son **temporales** ("you can remove them more inexpensively with restoration"). Un crítico de un drenador "inflicts twice the listed number of negative levels" (p. ej. un wraith mítico: 2 → 4 en un crítico).

**Respuestas del Cleric 11:**
- **Death Ward** (4.º, estándar, toque/30 pies, 1 min/nivel = **11 minutos**): "immune to energy drain and any negative energy effects, including channeled negative energy. This spell does not remove negative levels that the subject has already gained, but it does remove the penalties from negative levels for the duration of its effect" + "+4 morale bonus on saves against all death spells and magical death effects. The subject is granted a save to negate such effects even if one is not normally allowed." → preventivo sobre los 2 tanques antes de un combate con no muertos (2 ranuras de 4.º o 1 ranura + Inspired Spell). **Inspired SÍ**.
- **Restoration** (4.º, **3 asaltos**, toque; "dispels temporary negative levels" con "diamond dust worth 100 gp"; uno permanente con 1.000 po y máximo uno por semana). 3 asaltos de lanzamiento = **no es un conjuro de combate** (3 turnos sin actuar, provoca en cada uno si está amenazado); usarlo al terminar el combate, **antes de las 24 h**, para que ningún nivel llegue a la salvación de permanencia. No admite Inspired Spell (casting time > 1 estándar); sí **Recalled Blessing** (roll twice no aplica: no hay prueba). Pergaminos: 4 × 7 × 25 = 700 + 100 = **800 po** (temporales); 700 + 1.000 = **1.700 po** (uno permanente).
- **Mythic Heal augmented** (2 poderes): la única *restoration* de 1 acción estándar; solo si se conoce *mythic heal*.
- **Ward Against Death (Su)** (dominio Repose, 8.º nivel: https://www.aonprd.com/DomainDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Repose, Core pg. 46): "At 8th level, you can emit a 30-foot aura that wards against death for a number of rounds per day equal to your cleric level [11]. Living creatures in this area are immune to all death effects, energy drain, and effects that cause negative levels. This ward does not remove negative levels that a creature has already gained, but the negative levels have no effect while the creature is inside the warded area. These rounds do not need to be consecutive." Es la respuesta de grupo, pero exige el dominio Repose (deidades como Pharasma) en lugar de uno de los dos elegidos; solo si el frente 02 lo admite.
- **Lo que NO puede**: quitar más de un nivel permanente por semana, ni todos a la vez (*greater restoration*, 7.º, 5.000 po: pergamino 7.275 po con prueba DC 14 al 90 %).

### 2.b Daño, penalizador y drenaje de característica

**Regla** (https://aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=416, Core pg. 555): "For every 2 points of damage you take to a single ability, apply a –1 penalty to skills and statistics listed with the relevant ability. If the amount of ability damage you have taken equals or exceeds your ability score, you immediately fall unconscious until the damage is less than your ability score. The only exception to this is your Constitution score. If the damage to your Constitution is equal to or greater than your Constitution score, you die. Unless otherwise noted, damage to your ability scores is healed at the rate of 1 per day to each ability score that has been damaged." Con: "multiply your total Hit Dice by this penalty and subtract that amount from your current and total hit points" (un tanque de 11 DG con 6 de daño a Con: −3 × 11 = **−33 pg**). Penalizador (p. ej. *ray of enfeeblement*): "cannot cause you to fall unconscious or die [...] penalties cannot decrease your ability score to less than 1". Drenaje: "Ability drain actually reduces the relevant ability score [...] Ability drain can be healed through the use of spells such as restoration."

**Respuestas:** daño → *heal* (todo el daño de característica, 1 estándar), *lesser restoration* (2.º, **3 asaltos**, "1d4 points of temporary ability damage to one of the subject's ability scores"; varita CL 1 versión paladín 750 po: 50 cargas de 1d4 fuera de combate, 3 asaltos por carga: "If the spell being cast has a longer casting time than 1 action, however, it takes that long to cast the spell from a wand", Core pg. 496), *restoration* (3 asaltos, 100 po: "cures all temporary ability damage"), curas míticas (Mythic CLW/CMW/CSW/CCW curan "up to 1/2/3/4 points of ability damage", MA pg. 89: solo si se conocen). **Drenaje** → solo **Restoration** (100 po: "restores all points permanently drained from a single ability score (your choice if more than one is drained)"; una característica por lanzamiento, 3 asaltos) o *greater restoration* (todas; 7.º). **Greater Neutralize Poison** (cleric 6, 1 estándar, Dirty Tactics Toolbox pg. 28: "You automatically succeed at all caster level checks [...] also reverses all instantaneous or permanent effects caused by poisons, such as temporary ability damage or permanent ability drain (it does not heal ability drain caused by anything other than poison)") es la única cura de drenaje en 1 acción, solo para venenos. Con 0 = muerte (raise dead lo sube a 1); Wis/Str/Dex 0 = inconsciente hasta curar 1 punto (un CLW mítico o 3 asaltos de LR); Heal check "Treat Poison/Disease" da +4 a las salvaciones del aliado (Core pg. 98).

### 2.c PETRIFICACIÓN

**Stone to Flesh NO es de clérigo**: "Level arcanist 6, magus 6, shaman 6, sorcerer 6, witch 6, wizard 6" (https://www.aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Stone%20to%20Flesh, Core pg. 350); además "The creature must make a DC 15 Fortitude save to survive the process." *Flesh to Stone* (Core pg. 284): "Level arcanist 6, magus 6, shaman 6, sorcerer 6, witch 6, wizard 6", "Duration instantaneous", "The creature is not dead, but it does not seem to be alive either when viewed with spells such as deathwatch." Petrified (Conditions pg. 567): "considered unconscious. If a petrified character cracks or breaks, but the broken pieces are joined with the body as he returns to flesh, he is unharmed. If the character's petrified body is incomplete when it returns to flesh, the body is likewise incomplete and there is some amount of permanent hit point loss and/or debilitation."

**Break Enchantment** (https://www.aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Break%20Enchantment, Core pg. 251; cleric 5; **1 minuto**; V S; close 50 pies; "up to one creature per level, all within 30 ft. of each other"; SR no): "This spell frees victims from enchantments, transmutations, and curses. **Break enchantment can reverse even an instantaneous effect.** For each such effect, you make a caster level check (1d20 + caster level, maximum +15) against a DC of 11 + caster level of the effect. Success means that the creature is free of the spell, curse, or effect. For a cursed magic item, the DC is equal to the DC of the curse. **If the spell is one that cannot be dispelled by dispel magic or stone to flesh, break enchantment works only if that spell is 5th level or lower.** If the effect comes from a permanent magic item, break enchantment does not remove the curse from the item, but it does free the victim from the item's effects." → *flesh to stone* es de 6.º pero **sí** lo revierte *stone to flesh*, así que la limitación de 5.º **no aplica**: Break Enchantment funciona contra petrificación de cualquier nivel. El tope "maximum +15" no afecta a CL 11 (ni a CL 13).

**Mythic Break Enchantment** (MA pg. 87): "Against non-mythic effects, the spell automatically succeeds without requiring a caster level check. Against mythic effects, add your tier to your caster level check. The maximum level of spell this spell can remove is 5 + half your tier [5 + 1 = 6.º a tier 3]." → 1 poder + ranura de 5.º + conocer el mítico = **despetrificación garantizada** contra medusas, basiliscos, gorgonas y magos no míticos; sigue siendo 1 minuto de lanzamiento (fuera de combate).

**CL de una mirada sobrenatural**: Core pg. 221/554 (https://www.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?Name=Special+Abilities&Category=Magic+Basics) y la regla Gaze (https://www.aonprd.com/UMR.aspx?ItemName=Gaze) **no fijan un nivel de lanzador para las habilidades (Su)**; Table 16-1 solo dice que las Su no se disipan ("Dispel: No"). Break Enchantment no "disipa" sino que revierte el efecto y necesita un "caster level of the effect": la convención de mesa (y de guías no oficiales) es **CL = DG del monstruo** → **NO VERIFICADO en AoN, decisión del GM**. ### 2.d MALDICIONES COMPUESTAS

**Bestow Curse** (https://www.aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Bestow%20Curse, Core pg. 247; cleric 3; toque; Will negates): "–6 decrease to an ability score (to a minimum of 1); –4 penalty on attack rolls, saves, ability checks, and skill checks; each turn, the target has a 50% chance to act normally; otherwise, it takes no action." "The curse bestowed by this spell cannot be dispelled, but it can be removed with a break enchantment, limited wish, miracle, remove curse, or wish spell." **Remove Curse** (https://www.aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Remove%20Curse, Core pg. 332; cleric 3; **1 estándar**; toque; Will negates (harmless)): "If the target is a creature, you must make a caster level check (1d20 + caster level) against the DC of each curse affecting the target. Success means that the curse is removed. Remove curse does not remove the curse from a cursed shield, weapon, or suit of armor, although a successful caster level check enables the creature afflicted with any such cursed item to remove and get rid of it." **Diferencia con 3.5**: en PF1 *remove curse* **tiene prueba de CL contra la DC de la maldición** (y *remove disease* / *neutralize poison* también). Curses (https://www.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?Name=Curses&Category=Special%20Abilities, Core pg. 556): "most curses cannot be cured through a number of successful saving throws. Curses can be cured through magic, however, usually via spells such as remove curse and break enchantment." Un *bestow curse* de un clérigo enemigo de nivel 11 con Wis 20: DC 10 + 3 + 5 = 18 → 70 % (91 % con Eldritch Breach). Remove Curse es de 1 acción estándar → **Inspired Spell SÍ** (CL 13 → 80 %) o Recalled Blessing (roll twice → 91 %).

**Mummy rot** (https://www.aonprd.com/MonsterDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Mummy, Bestiary pg. 210; Mummy CR 5, 8 DG): "Curse and disease—slam; save Fort DC 16; onset 1 minute; frequency 1/day; effect 1d6 Con and 1d6 Cha; cure —. Mummy rot is both a curse and disease and can only be cured if the curse is first removed, at which point the disease can be magically removed. Even after the curse element of mummy rot is lifted, a creature suffering from it cannot recover naturally over time. Anyone casting a conjuration (healing) spell on the afflicted creature must succeed on a DC 20 caster level check, or the spell is wasted and the healing has no effect. Anyone who dies from mummy rot turns to dust and cannot be raised without a resurrection or greater magic." **Orden obligatorio**: (1) **Remove Curse** (abjuración → **no** paga la prueba DC 20; prueba de CL vs DC 16: 80 %; con Eldritch Breach 96 %); (2) **Remove Disease** (conjuración (curación) → primero DC 20 (60 %) y luego prueba vs DC 16 (80 %): **48 % combinado**; con Eldritch Breach 84 % × 96 % = **81 %**; CL 13: 63 %) **o Heal** (conjuración (curación): DC 20 → 60 % [84 % con Eldritch Breach] y termina "diseased" sin más prueba; además cura 110 y el daño de Con/Cha); (3) el daño de Con/Cha ya sufrido: *heal* (incluido en el paso 2 si se usó *heal*), *lesser restoration* o *restoration*. Mientras la maldición esté activa, **toda** cura de conjuración (curación) (*cure*, *heal*, *breath of life*, *restoration*) se pierde con 40 %: **channel energy no es un conjuro** → es la cura fiable sobre un afligido. Mummies de CR 10-13 (variantes de AP #81 con daño a Dex/Con/Cha, o avanzadas) suben la DC (Cha-based).

**Licantropía** (https://www.aonprd.com/MonsterTemplates.aspx?ItemName=Lycanthrope, Bestiary pg. 197): "Curse of Lycanthropy (Su): A natural lycanthrope's bite attack in animal or hybrid form infects a humanoid target with lycanthropy (Fortitude DC 15 negates)." Cura: "A remove disease or heal spell cast by a **cleric of 12th level or higher** cures the affliction, provided the character receives the spell within 3 days of the infecting lycanthrope's attack. Alternatively, consuming a dose of wolfsbane (Pathfinder RPG Core Rulebook 560) gives an afflicted lycanthrope a new Fortitude save to recover from lycanthropy." → **IMPOSIBLE con conjuros a nivel 11**: es nivel de clase, no CL (Inspired Spell da CL 13, no "cleric of 12th level"; **GM**). No hay DC de prueba de CL ni plazo en días más allá de los 3. Aunque se llama "Curse", el texto **no** menciona *remove curse* ni *break enchantment* → usarlos es **decisión del GM** (muchas mesas lo aceptan con prueba vs DC 15). Plan real hasta nivel 12: **Wolfsbane** (https://www.aonprd.com/PoisonDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Wolfsbane, Core pg. 560 / UE pg. 111): ingerido, **500 po**, "Fortitude DC 16; Onset 10 minutes; Frequency 1/minute for 6 minutes; Effect 1d3 Con; Cure 1 save" → la nueva salvación contra la licantropía es la Fort DC 15 del template; el veneno se anula después con *neutralize poison* (DC 16: 80 %) y el daño de Con con *heal*. Comprar **2 dosis (1.000 po)**. A nivel 12: *remove disease* preparado los días de hombres lobo.

**Ghoul fever** (https://www.aonprd.com/MonsterDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Ghoul, Bestiary pg. 146): "Bite—injury; save Fort DC 13; onset 1 day; frequency 1/day; effect 1d3 Con and 1d3 Dex damage; cure 2 consecutive saves." "A humanoid who dies of ghoul fever rises as a ghoul at the next midnight." **Remove Disease** (cleric 3, 1 estándar): "You must make a caster level check (1d20 + caster level) against the DC of each disease affecting the target" → DC 13: 2+ → **95 %** (100 % con Eldritch Breach); **Heal** termina "diseased" **sin prueba de CL** (ninguna en su texto) → contra enfermedades de DC alta (CR 12: DC 21 → *remove disease* 55 %) *heal* es la cura segura, a cambio de una ranura de 6.º. Diseases (Core pg. 557): "Most diseases can be cured by a number of consecutive saving throws or by spells such as remove disease." Otras enfermedades sobrenaturales de no muertos (p. ej. la de los ghasts, DC más alta) siguen la misma lógica. El daño de Con/Dex acumulado se cura aparte (*heal* lo cubre).

### 2.e MIEMBROS Y ÓRGANOS PERDIDOS

**Regenerate** (https://www.aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Regenerate, Core pg. 331): "**Level cleric 7**, druid 9, oracle 7, shaman 7, witch 7"; "Casting Time 3 full rounds"; "The subject's severed body members (fingers, toes, hands, feet, arms, legs, tails, or even heads of multiheaded creatures), broken bones, and ruined organs grow back. [...] complete in 1 round if the severed members are present and touching the creature. It takes 2d10 rounds otherwise. Regenerate also cures 4d8 points of damage + 1 point per caster level (maximum +35), rids the subject of exhaustion and fatigue". **No lanzable a nivel 11** (7.º). **Remove Blindness/Deafness** (https://www.aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Remove%20Blindness%2FDeafness, Core pg. 332; cleric 3): "cures blindness or deafness (your choice), whether the effect is normal or magical in nature. **The spell does not restore ears or eyes that have been lost, but it repairs them if they are damaged.**" → ceguera por ojos arrancados (críticos de las Critical Hit Deck, maldiciones, *vorpal*): ni *heal* ni RB/D; solo *regenerate*. **Pergamino de regenerate**: 7 × 13 × 25 = **2.275 po**, activable por un CL 11 con prueba **DC 14** (13 + 1) → 90 % (99 % con Surge); el fallo sin mishap (Wis check DC 5) no consume el pergamino; 3 asaltos completos de lectura. NPC: 910 po en una metrópolis. **Mythic Regenerate** (MA pg. 105) baja el tiempo a 1 acción estándar y revive decapitados hace ≤ 1 asalto "as breath of life", pero exige lanzar el conjuro (7.º) → fuera de alcance hasta nivel 13. Nota de *raise dead*: "the body of the creature to be raised must be whole. Otherwise, missing parts are still missing" → un aliado decapitado por *vorpal* vuelve con *raise dead* **sin cabeza**... es decir, no vuelve: hace falta *regenerate* después (o *resurrection*, que ignora el estado del cuerpo).

## SECCIÓN 3 — CURAR EN CUERPO A CUERPO Y DESCANSO

### 3.1 Qué provoca y qué no (Core pg. 180-185)

- **Attacks of Opportunity** (https://www.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?Name=Attacks%20of%20Opportunity&Category=Combat, Core pg. 180): "Two kinds of actions can provoke attacks of opportunity: moving out of a threatened square and performing certain actions within a threatened square." "There are two common methods of avoiding such an attack—the 5-foot step and the withdraw action." "An attack of opportunity is a single melee attack, and most characters can only make one per round."
- **Cast a Spell** (https://aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=133, Core pg. 183): "Generally, if you cast a spell, you provoke attacks of opportunity from threatening enemies. If you take damage from an attack of opportunity, you must make a concentration check (**DC 10 + points of damage taken + the spell's level**) or lose the spell. Spells that require only a swift action to cast don't provoke attacks of opportunity." "Casting on the Defensive: [...] does not provoke an attack of opportunity. It does, however, require a concentration check (DC 15 + double the spell's level)." Cuentas: *heal* (6.º) tras un AdO de 18 de daño: DC 34 vs +18 → 25 %; a la defensiva DC 27 → 60 % (80 % con Combat Casting; 78 % con Surge). Conclusión: **amenazado, nunca "tragarse" el AdO con un conjuro de 5.º-6.º**.
- **Touch Spells in Combat** (misma página, Core pg. 185): "In the same round that you cast the spell, you may also touch (or attempt to touch) as a free action. [...] **You can automatically touch one friend or use the spell on yourself, but to touch an opponent, you must succeed on an attack roll.**" "Touch Attacks: Touching an opponent with a touch spell is considered to be an armed attack and therefore does not provoke attacks of opportunity. **The act of casting a spell, however, does provoke an attack of opportunity.**" "Holding the Charge: [...] You can touch one friend as a standard action or up to six friends as a full-round action." "Ranged Touch Spells in Combat: [...] Ranged touch attacks provoke an attack of opportunity, even if the spell that causes the attacks was cast defensively." → Tocar a un aliado voluntario: automático y sin AdO; lo que provoca es **lanzar** el conjuro estando amenazado.
- **Table 8-2 Actions in Combat** (https://www.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?Name=Actions%20in%20Combat&Category=Combat, Core pg. 183; AoN la rotula "Table 7-2"), columna "Attack of Opportunity": Cast a spell (1 standard action) **Yes** · **Channel energy No** · Drink a potion **Yes** · **Read a scroll Yes** · Use spell-like ability **Yes** (Rebuke Death) · Use supernatural ability **No** · Stabilize a dying friend (Heal skill) **Yes** · Deliver coup de grace **Yes** · Use a touch spell on up to six friends **Yes** · 5-foot step **No**.
- **Activate Magic Item** (misma página, Core pg. 183): "Spell Completion Items: Activating a spell completion item [pergamino] is the equivalent of casting a spell. It requires concentration and provokes attacks of opportunity. [...] you can attempt to activate the item while on the defensive, as with casting a spell." "Spell Trigger, Command Word, or Use-Activated Items: Activating any of these kinds of items does not require concentration and **does not provoke attacks of opportunity**." Wands (Core pg. 496): "Wands use the spell trigger activation method, so casting a spell from a wand is usually a standard action that doesn't provoke attacks of opportunity. [...] If the spell being cast has a longer casting time than 1 action, however, it takes that long to cast the spell from a wand." Using Items (Core pg. 458): "Spell trigger items can be used by anyone whose class can cast the corresponding spell" → varita de CLW y de *lesser restoration* (paladín 1 está en la lista de clérigo como 2.º: vale, el conjuro está en la lista) sin tirada y sin AdO.
- **Channel Energy** (https://www.aonprd.com/ClassDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Cleric, Core pg. 40): "This is a standard action that does not provoke an attack of opportunity." Quick Channel (acción de movimiento por 2 usos) es la misma habilidad (Su): tampoco provoca.
- **Faith's Reach (Su)** (MA pg. 34): "Whenever you cast a divine spell with a range of touch, you can instead cast the spell with a range of 30 feet. If the spell normally requires a melee touch attack, it instead requires a ranged touch attack." Lo que cambia: ya no hace falta estar adyacente al aliado. Lo que **no** cambia: **lanzar sigue provocando si el clérigo está amenazado** (no es un conjuro rápido). Sobre un aliado voluntario el conjuro **no** "requires a melee touch attack" (el toque es automático, Core pg. 185) → lectura mayoritaria: no hay tirada ni AdO extra; lectura estricta: ataque de toque a distancia (+9: BAB 8 + Dex 1) contra la CA de toque del aliado, que **provoca otro AdO** y falla con 1 natural (5 %). **Sin FAQ oficial → pactar con el GM por escrito** (el frente 12-supervivencia ya lo listó; sigue abierto).
- **Combat Casting** (+4 a la defensiva/agarrado) y **Warrior Priest** (+2 y +1 iniciativa) ya cuantificados en el frente 04: no se repiten.

### 3.2 Recuperación del poder mítico y descanso

**Texto exacto** (https://www.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=1618, MA pg. 12): "**Mythic Power (Su):** Mythic characters can draw upon a wellspring of power to accomplish amazing deeds and cheat fate. This power is used by a number of different abilities. **Each day**, you can expend an amount of mythic power equal to 3 plus double your mythic tier (5/day at 1st tier, 7/day at 2nd, etc.). This amount is your maximum amount of mythic power. If an ability allows you to regain uses of your mythic power, you can never have more than this amount." → AoN **no fija hora ni exige 8 h de descanso**: solo "each day". Únicas recargas explícitas: **Legendary Hero** (tier 10: "one use per hour, in addition to completely refreshing your uses each day"), Divine Vessel (Hierophant tier 10), Mythic Boons del GM. **Decisión del GM**: proponer que el poder mítico se recargue en la **hora de rezo** del clérigo, que es su reloj diario de conjuros.

**Recuperation (Ex)** (misma URL): "At 3rd tier, you are restored to full hit points after 8 hours of rest so long as you aren't dead. In addition, by expending one use of mythic power and resting for 1 hour, you regain a number of hit points equal to half your full hit points (up to a maximum of your full hit points) and regain the use of any class features that are limited to a certain number of uses per day (such as barbarian rage, bardic performance, spells per day, and so on). This rest is treated as 8 hours of sleep for such abilities. **This rest doesn't refresh uses of mythic power or any mythic abilities that are limited to a number of times per day.**" → devuelve **channel energy y conjuros/día** (explícito) pero **nunca poder mítico**; los 9 poderes son el presupuesto del día entero.

**Preparación divina** (https://www.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?Name=Preparing%20Divine%20Spells&Category=Divine%20Spells, Core pg. 220): "a divine spellcaster [...] does not require a period of rest to prepare spells. Instead, the character chooses a particular time of day to pray and receive spells. [...] If the character does not stop to pray for spells at the first opportunity, she must wait until the next day to prepare spells." "The time required to prepare spells is the same as it is for a wizard (1 hour), as is the requirement for a relatively peaceful environment." "**Recent Casting Limit:** As with arcane spells, at the time of preparation any spells cast within the previous 8 hours count against the number of spells that can be prepared." Cruce con Recuperation: la hora de descanso "is treated as 8 hours of sleep for such abilities" y "regain [...] spells per day" → lectura razonable: tras Recuperation se puede hacer una sesión de preparación (1 h) sin que los conjuros de las últimas 8 h cuenten (si contaran, Recuperation no recargaría nada); el reloj de la **hora de rezo** sigue siendo el del GM ("she must wait until the next day"): **decisión del GM** si acepta un rezo extraordinario después de Recuperation. Sustained by Faith (tier 1: "If you have abilities or class features that require rest before they can be regained, you can choose to regain them once per day by spending 1 hour in uninterrupted meditation") cubre literalmente lo que "require rest" (los conjuros de clérigo no) → GM.

## ADVERTENCIAS / DESINFORMACIÓN FRECUENTE

1. **"Se muere a −10"**: D&D 3.5. En PF1 es **−Con** (Core pg. 190), y **−2×Con** con Hard to Kill.
2. **"Estabilizar es 10 %"**: 3.5. En PF1 es una **prueba de Con DC 10 con penalizador igual a los pg negativos**, 20 natural = éxito, autofallo con bleed.
3. **"Remove curse / remove disease / neutralize poison curan automáticamente"**: 3.5. En PF1 los tres exigen **prueba de CL (1d20 + CL) contra la DC** de la aflicción. *Heal* sí quita "diseased"/"poisoned" sin prueba (pero no maldiciones).
4. **"Raise Dead da 1 nivel negativo"**: 3.5 (perdía un nivel). En PF1 son **2 niveles negativos permanentes** y 1.000 po + 1 semana por cada uno con *restoration*; *resurrection* da 1.
5. **"Breath of life revive a cualquiera"**: no vs "death effects", no si queda a −Con o peor, no a no muertos, no a muertos hace 2+ asaltos (salvo el mítico).
6. **"Death ward protege del coup de grace / del daño masivo"**: no: "does not protect against other sorts of attacks, even if those attacks might be lethal".
7. **"Energy drain es un death effect"**: FAQ oficial: **no**. Death Ward inmuniza por su texto explícito, no por ser death effect.
8. **"Los niveles negativos se vuelven permanentes a las 24 h automáticamente"**: no: a las 24 h hay una **salvación de Fort** por nivel (UMR Energy Drain); la FAQ 2017 confirma que son temporales hasta entonces (*restoration* de 100 po).
9. **"Restoration se lanza en combate"**: 3 asaltos de lanzamiento (también *lesser* y *greater*): no es de combate; Inspired Spell no la acelera; Mythic Heal augmented es la única de 1 acción.
10. **"Stone to flesh está en la lista del clérigo"**: **no** (arcano 6.º). Break Enchantment sí revierte la petrificación (incluida *flesh to stone* de 6.º).
11. **"Un clérigo puede leer un pergamino arcano"**: solo con UMD (DC 20 + CL = 31); no es habilidad de clase.
12. **"Massive damage es regla base"**: es "(Optional Rule)".
13. **"Cheat Death sirve para salvar a un aliado"**: "but not another character or NPC".
14. **"El poder mítico vuelve al dormir 8 h"**: AoN solo dice "each day"; Recuperation **no** lo recarga.
15. **d20pfsrd**: mezcla 3PP (p. ej. dotes de curación de terceros) y renombra deidades; solo como pista. **2e.aonprd.com** (Pathfinder 2e) tiene una *breath of life* distinta (reacción, 4d8+): no usar.
16. **Lo NO VERIFICADO en AoN de este dossier**: el CL de una mirada (Su) para la DC de Break Enchantment (convención DG); las dos lecturas del umbral de BoL sobre míticos; si Relentless Healing impone nivel negativo o funciona vs death effects; si Mythic Heal augmented exige el polvo de diamante; la lectura del toque a distancia sobre aliados voluntarios (Faith's Reach); si Inspired Spell cuenta como "cleric of 12th level" para la licantropía.
